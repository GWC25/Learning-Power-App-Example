# Using AI to simplify dense text

*How-to guide · Resource Files library*

## What it is

Copilot can rewrite a paragraph or document in plainer, shorter language while keeping the original meaning.

## Why use it

A plain-language version is faster to produce with AI than rewriting by hand, and helps learners with reading barriers access the same content.

## How to

1. Paste the text into Word or Copilot Chat.
2. Ask it to 'Rewrite this in plain English' and describe your audience, such as reading level or first language.
3. Read the simplified version against the original, check nothing important has been lost or changed in meaning.
4. Adjust anything that still sounds too formal or complex.
5. Offer both versions where possible, rather than replacing the original entirely.
