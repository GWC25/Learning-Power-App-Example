# Checking AI output before you use it

*How-to guide · Resource Files library*

## What it is

AI tools like Copilot can produce confident-sounding text that is factually wrong, out of date, or invented, sometimes called a 'hallucination.'

## Why use it

Checking before you use it protects you, your learners, and the college from acting on something incorrect.

## How to

1. Never send, publish or submit AI-generated content without reading it fully first.
2. Check any names, dates, figures, policies or quotes against a source you trust, don't assume they're correct.
3. Ask yourself whether the tone and content are right for who's receiving it.
4. If AI drafted something from a document you pasted in, check nothing important was left out.
5. Treat it as a first draft from a keen but occasionally unreliable colleague, not a finished, checked piece of work.
