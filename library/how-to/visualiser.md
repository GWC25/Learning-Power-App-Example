# Using a visualiser to show your screen or workspace

*How-to guide · Resource Files library*

## What it is

A visualiser is a camera device that projects whatever is placed underneath it, paper, a whiteboard, an object, onto a screen in real time.

## Why use it

It lets you show live working, such as handwriting, diagrams or physical materials, to a whole room or a screen-share without writing everything onto a shared digital surface first. It works with material you already use, so nothing separate has to be prepared in advance.

## How to

1. Connect the visualiser to your teaching station via USB or HDMI, depending on the model.
2. Select the visualiser as the video source in your display or meeting software.
3. Place what you want to show underneath the camera and adjust focus or zoom as needed.
4. If recording for later reuse, start your screen recording tool before you begin.
