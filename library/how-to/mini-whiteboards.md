# Using mini whiteboards to check understanding

*How-to guide · Resource Files library*

## What it is

Mini whiteboards are small individual whiteboards, one per learner, that they write or draw an answer on and hold up for you to see.

## Why use it

They give you an instant, visible check of every learner's answer at the same time, with no device, login or setup needed. You can see straight away who needs support before moving on.

## How to

1. Give each learner a mini whiteboard, a dry-wipe pen, and something to wipe it with, such as a cloth or tissue.
2. Ask a question that has a short answer, for example a word, a number, or a short phrase.
3. Give learners a short, fixed amount of time to write their answer.
4. Say "boards up" (or your own agreed signal) so everyone shows their answer at the same time.
5. Scan the room for correct answers, common mistakes, and anyone who has left their board blank.
6. Ask learners to wipe their board clean before the next question.
