# Immersive Reader

*How-to guide · Resource Files library*

This guide is also the text alternative for the short video [immersive-reader.mp4](../videos/immersive-reader.mp4), which has no sound.

## The problem

A long, dense page is hard work. Some learners give up before the second line, not because the content is too hard, but because the reading is.

## Why it matters for learners

- Learners with dyslexia, visual stress or low vision.
- Learners reading in a second or third language.
- Anyone reading at the end of a long day.

Immersive Reader lets each learner choose how they read, without you making a separate version.

## How to fix it

1. In **Word**: go to **View**, then **Immersive Reader**.
2. In **Teams**: on a message or assignment, choose **More options**, then **Immersive Reader**.
3. It also works in OneNote, Outlook and the Microsoft Edge browser.

What learners can do in it:

- Press play to **hear it read aloud**, with each word highlighted.
- Change **text size, spacing and background colour**.
- Use **Line Focus** to show one, three or five lines at a time.
- Use **Translate** and the picture dictionary.

## Make it work well

Immersive Reader works best on a document built properly: real headings, alt text and a sensible reading order. Run the Accessibility Checker first.

## Check it

Open your own resource in Immersive Reader and press play. Does it read in a sensible order? Do the headings make sense on their own?

Show learners where it is, and let them choose when to use it.
