# Using Microsoft Forms to check understanding

*How-to guide · Resource Files library*

## What it is

Microsoft Forms is a Microsoft 365 tool for building a short online form or quiz that people fill in from a link or QR code, with the results collected automatically.

## Why use it

It lets you check what a whole class understands in a couple of minutes, without collecting or marking anything on paper. The same form also works as an exit ticket, a short check learners complete shortly before they leave the room, so you know what to revisit next lesson.

## How to

1. Go to forms.office.com and sign in with your college Microsoft 365 account.
2. Select + New Form.
3. Give the form a title, for example "End of lesson check".
4. Select + Add new to add a question, and choose a question type such as Choice or Text.
5. Repeat the previous step for each question you want to ask.
6. Select Collect responses (or the Share icon) to get a link or QR code.
7. Share the link or QR code with learners so they can respond on their own device.
8. Select Responses at the top of the form to see results as they come in.
