# Using Plickers for a class poll without learner devices

*How-to guide · Resource Files library*

## What it is

Plickers is a free app and set of printed response cards that let you poll a class and collect answers using only your own phone, learners don't need a device.

## Why use it

It gives you the speed of a digital poll in rooms where learners don't have devices, or where you'd rather they didn't use one. Each learner holds up their card.

## How to

1. Download the Plickers app on your phone and create a free account at plickers.com.
2. Print a set of Plickers cards from the website, each card has a unique pattern and is numbered for a specific learner or seat.
3. In the app or on plickers.com, add a class and build a question, for example a multiple-choice question with up to four answers.
4. Give each learner their numbered card.
5. Display the question on the board or screen so learners can see the answer options.
6. Ask learners to hold up their card, turned so the letter matching their answer is at the top.
7. Open the question in the Plickers app and use your phone's camera to scan the room, the app reads each card automatically.
8. Check the live results shown in the app or on the shared screen.
